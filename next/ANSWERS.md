Extracted: (1) the origin is honestly unknown, like ours; (2) trapped by need, not walls: a community bound to the fire, which punishes free riders; (3) light against dark as the frame; (4) the dark is indifferent: entropy, not malice; (5) the game tells the truth: the odds never favor you; (6) feeling real over being real; (7) the mirror realm of an ordinary Earth day.

Proposed (pending his answers): everyone remembers the same ordinary Tuesday, then the dark; the camp runs like a cheerful town (commute into the dark, shifts at the fire, a school for the maze, "Daily light quota. Have a nice day."); the fire is a commons and the community cuts free riders out of its light; the dark is the maze, walled by odds; decay, diminishing returns and no forever-win are in the rules.

## The look (2026-10-19 about 01:50)
- Which base look (painterly like Arcane, grounded like Icarus, sci-fi awe like Destiny, a blend by era)? "this is actually my hardest decisons because it needs eyes on". Three rough versions of the same scene are being made for his eyes.
- Where the neon lives: everywhere, the camp's tech, the light network, the dark's strangeness, the sky.
- The look, his answer (2026-10-19 about 02:00): "Whatever would make the world feel the most immersive. I want to reduce the amount of time that I'm not feeling immersed in the real thing, feeling everything feel real as I'm playing it or experiencing it. leaning sci fi awe tho". **Decided: grounded near, awe far.** Within arm's reach everything is believable (real materials, light that behaves like light, sound from where things are); in the distance, sci-fi awe (the sky, the network's beams, the dark's impossible colors); no UI between him and the world. The closer, the more real; the farther, the more wondrous.
