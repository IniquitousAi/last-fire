# What Daniel wants Last Fire to be (his answers, lowest layer first)

How these are asked (his rule, 2026-10-18 about 22:00): only about the why (the feelings, the reasons), never the what. Claude invents the mechanics from the answers and the research. Ask until the only bottleneck is building it.
