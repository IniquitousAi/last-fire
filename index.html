HOW IT WORKS

Every night is a round. When the sun goes down the Hollow come out of the dark, more and tougher each night. Live till dawn: sunlight burns them away.

Light is everything. They cannot stand firelight: they burn at its edge and walk around it. In the dark they tear walls apart; a lit wall holds. A big fire calls them from far off. Keep your fires fed with planks.

They wear armor. Pale plates on the head and chest take most of a hit until they crack. You hear a hard ping on armor and a crack when a plate breaks (20 points). The M14 and the RPK punch through armor best, the pistol worst.

Four kinds. Hollow walk at you. Runners (from night 3) are low and fast. Shards (night 4) float to your fires and eat the light: shoot them first. Giants (night 5) are slow, heavily armored and walk straight through firelight and walls.

Points buy everything. 10 a hit, 60 a kill, 100 a headshot kill. Spend them at the base (the walled yard where you start): guns on the walls, perk machines (pink). A kill sometimes leaves a drop: walk over it and everyone gets it.

Together. If you go down, a friend holds F next to you within 30 seconds to bring you back. Alone, you get up by the fire after 8 seconds and lose 10% of your points.

PLAYING WITH FRIENDS

The host clicks HOST A GAME, presses Esc and clicks COPY INVITE, then pastes the link in Discord. Friends click the link, type a name and click JOIN. Up to 4 players. The host can lock the room or remove a player from the Esc menu. When the host leaves, the game ends for everyone.
