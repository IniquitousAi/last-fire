LAST FIRE

The dark comes every night. Fire keeps it out.

Move WASD, Shift to run, Space to jump. Left click shoot or mine, right click aim (or place a block, or feed a fire). R reloads, F buys or revives. 1 to 8 or the wheel pick what you hold. C crafts a campfire from 4 planks, E eats.
Every night is a round. The Hollow come, a little tougher each night: every hit earns points, and points buy guns and perks at the base. They cannot stand firelight, they take apart walls left in the dark, and a big fire calls them from far away. Every 7th night is the Long Night.
